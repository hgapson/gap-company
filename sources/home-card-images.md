# Homepage card image trial

Scope: only the three cards in the second homepage section. Other cards retain their existing design.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then optimised to 960-pixel JPEG files. These are illustrative scenes; the software screens are concepts rather than screenshots of available GAP TECH products.

## it-support-card

Saved asset: `public/images/it-support-card.jpg`

Prompt: Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create one landscape 3:2 editorial photo for a GAP TECH homepage service card. Natural warm daylight, cream and sage workspace, subtle navy accents, light oak surfaces, approachable and professional, matching a friendly small-business IT website. Centre the subject so a wide 16:9 crop works. No readable text, logos, watermarks or text overlays. Close-up of a technician’s hands connecting a blue ethernet cable to a small office network switch beside a laptop, tidy realistic equipment, shallow depth of field.
